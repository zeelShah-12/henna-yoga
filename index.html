My Approach

We Start With Where It Hurts

Every session begins with a conversation, not a pose. What's paining you physically, and what's weighing on you mentally? From there, I build a plan that speaks to both.

1. Share Your Story

Tell me about your body — the knee, the back, the thyroid fatigue — and how you've been feeling emotionally.

2. A Personalized Plan

I design a therapeutic yoga and counselling plan around your specific pain points, physical and mental.

3. Practice & Support

Gentle sessions online, with ongoing check-ins — so the plan adjusts as you feel better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any yoga experience?

Not at all. Most of our students are complete beginners. Every class is taught at a gentle, easy-to-follow pace.

Do you work with thyroid or other lifestyle diseases?

Yes — thyroid imbalance, diabetes, and blood pressure are some of the most common conditions I work with, alongside heart, kidney, and joint concerns. We start with a conversation about what you're managing, and build your practice around it.

Is this yoga or counselling?

Both. Every session pairs therapeutic yoga with gentle counselling — we talk about where you're holding pain physically and how you've been feeling mentally, and the practice follows from there. It's not a substitute for medical or clinical mental health care, but a supportive complement to it.

Do you teach students outside the country?

Yes — most of our students join live online from around the world. We'll work with you to find a class time that fits your timezone.

What should I wear, and do I need a mat?

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing you can move in. A mat helps, but a sturdy chair works just as well.

Can I try a class before committing?

Yes — your first class is free. Just message us to reserve your spot.
